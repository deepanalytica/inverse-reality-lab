# References and Evidence Base

This bibliography separates **archaeological/empirical evidence** from **mathematical tools** and **relativistic comparison systems**. The presence of a reference in the latter categories does not make it archaeological evidence.

---

# A. Great Pyramid, Egyptian construction and archaeology

1. **Petrie, W. M. F. (1883).** *The Pyramids and Temples of Gizeh.* Field & Tuer, London.  
   Foundational metrical survey of the Giza pyramids and internal architecture.

2. **Arnold, D. (1991).** *Building in Egypt: Pharaonic Stone Masonry.* Oxford University Press.  
   Major reference on Egyptian stone construction, quarrying, transport and masonry practice.

3. **Lehner, M. (1997).** *The Complete Pyramids.* Thames & Hudson.  
   Synthetic Egyptological reference on pyramid complexes and construction context.

4. **Morishima, K. et al. (2017).** “Discovery of a big void in Khufu’s Pyramid by observation of cosmic-ray muons.” *Nature* 552, 386–390.  
   DOI: 10.1038/nature24647  
   https://www.nature.com/articles/nature24647  
   **Evidence role:** independently establishes a major density anomaly / void region via multiple muon-detector technologies.

5. **Procureur, S. et al. (2023).** “Precise characterization of a corridor-shaped structure in Khufu’s Pyramid by observation of cosmic-ray muons.” *Nature Communications* 14, 1144.  
   DOI: 10.1038/s41467-023-36351-0  
   https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-36351-0  
   **Evidence role:** constrains geometry/location of the north-face corridor through muography.

6. **Ancient Egypt Research Associates (AERA).** “The Great Pyramid Quarry.”  
   https://aeraweb.org/great-pyramid-quarry/  
   **Evidence role:** quarry geometry, extraction traces and material-volume context adjacent to the monument.

7. **Ghoneim, E. et al. (2024).** “The Egyptian pyramid chain was built along the now abandoned Ahramat Nile Branch.” *Communications Earth & Environment* 5, 233.  
   DOI: 10.1038/s43247-024-01379-7  
   https://www.nature.com/articles/s43247-024-01379-7  
   **Evidence role:** paleohydrological context relevant to regional transport/access.

# F. Evidence-status rule

The project uses the following rule:

\[
\boxed{
\text{citation to mathematics}
\neq
\text{archaeological evidence}.
}
\]

Published measurements, surveys, excavations and instrument observations can support historical claims. Mathematics determines what follows from assumptions; it cannot by itself establish that those assumptions describe Khufu.
