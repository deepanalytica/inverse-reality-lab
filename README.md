# Inverse Reality Laboratory (IRL)

**PRAXIOS / Meta-Harness research module** for evidence-constrained inverse causal reconstruction of physical objects from a terminal state.

The **Great Pyramid of Khufu** is the primary archaeological laboratory. Vaidya collapse and Kerr null-geodesic accessibility are used only as mathematical comparison systems for complement-defined regions, critical boundaries and changes of accessibility.

## Research question

Given a terminal physical state `X_T`, evidence `E`, and admissible physical laws `L`, infer a distribution over antecedent states, construction partial orders, negative topological spaces, material flows and candidate generative histories:

`P(G, T, M+, M⊖, K+, K-, A | X_T, E, L)`

The project **does not** claim antigravity, physical negative mass, literal backward-time evolution, or a physical equivalence between pyramids and black holes. "Inverse gravity" means reverse accounting of gravitational work/potential along an inferred inverse trajectory. "Negative mass" means **counterfactual absent mass** relative to a declared solid reference volume.

## Interactive laboratory

Open the public lab at **https://deepanalytica.github.io/inverse-reality-lab/** or open `index.html` locally. It includes:

- **Khufu inverse disassembly** — idealized reverse geometric front, mass removed, gravitational-potential release, and the topological identity of the King's Chamber as a negative object.
- **Vaidya collapse** — apparent horizon and numerically back-integrated event horizon in a didactic spherical-collapse model.
- **Kerr accessibility** — equatorial null-geodesic radial potential and a connectivity transition around the critical light ring.
- **Unified ontology view** — compares loss of accessibility without claiming identical physics.
