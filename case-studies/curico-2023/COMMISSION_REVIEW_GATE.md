# Commission review gate

A reviewer should attempt to falsify, not merely confirm.

## Review questions
1. Are event identities and timestamps defensible?
2. Are geometries authoritative or accompanied by uncertainty?
3. Does drainage topology permit each claimed physical connection?
4. Are common-cause and direct-cause explanations separated?
5. Are June and August treated with appropriate dependence?
6. Are MapBiomas resolution/classification limits respected?
7. Are rainfall/discharge values tied to named stations/products and QC?
8. Are remote-sensing changes distinguishable from seasonality/artifact?
9. Are infrastructure failures linked to documented assets and mechanisms?
10. Are spatial autocorrelation and MAUP addressed?
11. Are alternative hypotheses explicitly tested?
12. Can all central outputs be reproduced?
13. Could a skeptical specialist trace each headline claim to raw evidence?
14. Are negative/null findings preserved?
15. Does the public interface distinguish observation from inference?

## Severity
CRITICAL: invalidates a central claim/result or reproducibility.
MAJOR: materially changes interpretation.
MINOR: documentation/precision defect without material conclusion change.

## Release rule
No CRITICAL open findings. MAJOR findings must be resolved or explicitly scope-limiting. All review decisions are versioned.
