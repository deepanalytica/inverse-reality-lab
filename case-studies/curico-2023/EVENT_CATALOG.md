# Event and impact catalog — v0.1

## Regional forcing
- **June 21–26, 2023:** CIGIDEN reports ~85 h continuous precipitation and ~320 mm mean basin precipitation; high freezing level left most of the basin exposed to rainfall.
- **August 18–23, 2023:** CIGIDEN reports ~110 h duration and ~520 mm mean total basin precipitation; estimated liquid precipitation ~332 mm versus ~288 mm in June. Gauge failures introduce uncertainty.
- A Cámara document citing Junta de Vigilancia del Río Lontué reports Cordillerilla–Los Niches totals of 460.2 mm (June) and 246.4 mm (August), versus Curicó 276 mm and 46.2 mm. Reconcile station metadata before analytical use.

## Upeo / Monte Oscuro — 22–24 Aug
Observed: debris flows/landslides, road blockages, bridge losses, isolated population, helicopter access and emergency supply needs. Early reporting cited ~300 isolated people. Authorities reported >5 major landslides before Upeo Centro and multiple failures between Upeo Centro and Monte Oscuro.
Cascade: rainfall → slope/channel response → debris/landslide processes → road/bridge failure → isolation → medical/logistics response.
Status: impacts documented; source areas and causal edges require terrain/remote-sensing validation.

## Camino Las Piedras / Los Niches
Official Vialidad records for 19–20 Aug identify water over J-641 (Cordillerilla–San Jorge), sector Las Piedras, and J-647 Las Piedras–Interior. Later reporting describes asphalt removed/destroyed during the storms and prolonged mobility impacts.
Recovery records document emergency works on the Los Niches/Chequenlemu corridor: excavation, compacted embankments, protective gabions, subgrade, granular base, surface patching and marking.
Status: event and repair documented; exact erosional corridor/flow source pending geospatial reconstruction.

## Parque Zapallar — 21–22 Aug
SENAPRED evacuation due to Estero Guaiquillo overflow; flooded homes, emergency shelter and subsequent electricity-restoration inspection. Press cited roughly 400 affected homes; verify against official damage records.

## Dragones Sur
Evacuation ordered during Guaiquillo overflow; subsequent institutional requests addressed crossing infrastructure.

## Urban-planning aftermath
Municipal action after the floods included temporary restrictions affecting permits in identified inundated/risk sectors. Public controversy arose over prior urbanization/permissions in Parque Zapallar; competing claims remain attributed until planning files are reviewed.

## Critical non-claim
The catalog does **not** establish Upeo → Zapallar as a direct physical causal chain. Working model: branching cascade under common meteorological forcing, to be tested through basin topology, timing and hydrologic evidence.
