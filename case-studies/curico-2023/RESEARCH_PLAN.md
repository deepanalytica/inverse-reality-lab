# Research plan

## WP1 — Forensic catalog
Geocode every documented event, closure, bridge failure, evacuation, inundation and repair. Preserve source, time, geometry, confidence and provenance.

## WP2 — MapBiomas antecedent landscape
Use annual 1999–2023/24 coverage to calculate transitions by basin, sub-basin, riparian buffer and event corridor. Test urban expansion, forest/plantation/agriculture transitions, bare land, fire and recovery. MapBiomas is analytical input, not decorative context.

## WP3 — Terrain and network
Derive conditioned drainage, slope, curvature, flow accumulation, catchments and connectivity. Snap event nodes with uncertainty buffers.

## WP4 — Event forcing
Reconstruct June/August rainfall, temperature/freezing level and discharge. Treat June→August as a natural comparison; test antecedent damage/saturation rather than assuming it.

## WP5 — Earth observation
Sentinel-1 SAR for flood/change; Sentinel-2/Landsat for pre/post surface changes; identify candidate debris-flow/erosion corridors.

## WP6 — Infrastructure cascade
Roads J-641/J-645/J-647 and Upeo access; bridges/rail; Guaiquillo crossings; electricity. Link damage to works, contracts, costs and completion.

## WP7 — Causal graph
Build spatiotemporal DAG/event graph. No causal edge without proof obligation. Compare alternative explanations.

## WP8 — Validation
Independent hydrology/geomorphology/GIS review and field verification where feasible.

## Deliverables
Evidence registry; source registry; event graph; MapBiomas transition matrices; June/August comparison; interactive public demonstrator; technical report; reproducibility package.

## Public/private boundary
Public case contains evidence, reproducible analytical methods and derived results. Do not disclose proprietary PRAXIOS/Meta-Harness internals or unrelated Deep Geo architecture.
