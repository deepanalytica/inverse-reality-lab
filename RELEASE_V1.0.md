## Central correction introduced by v1.0

Early exploratory benchmarks sometimes used language implying that exact historical mechanisms had been recovered.

The adversarial study showed that the defensible hierarchy is:

\[
I0\rightarrow I1\rightarrow I2\rightarrow I3
\]

from coarse causal order to exact historical implementation.

Most terminal-state inference supports I0–I1. I2 requires additional evidence. I3 normally requires direct historical documentation.
